# Diagnostic

Run this phase only after intake has been imported and validated.

Assess prior knowledge with targeted questions or practical tasks. Do not rely only on self-declared labels such as beginner or advanced.

## Interaction style

- Keep the diagnostic proportional to the learner's goal, current level and weekly availability.
- Ask one short question or task at a time.
- Explain briefly why an answer needs clarification only when necessary.
- Do not present the entire questionnaire at once.
- Stop early when enough evidence exists to choose a responsible starting depth.
- Do not generate the curriculum during this phase.

Produce:

- confirmed competencies;
- knowledge gaps;
- misconceptions;
- required prerequisites;
- recommended starting depth.

The diagnostic may be skipped only when reliable existing evidence supports the starting level. Self-declaration alone is not sufficient evidence.

At completion, follow `instructions/phase-completion.md`. Summarize only the diagnostic conclusion and material caveats, then guide the owner to the generation phase with a command equivalent to:

`Gere uma proposta de trilha com base no intake e no diagnóstico. Abra um pull request e não publique tarefas ainda.`
